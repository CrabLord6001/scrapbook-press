Scrapbook Press — how it works

The point is that a page should take two minutes, not forty.

1. Pick a template for the page.
2. Add photos — tap Add photo on any empty zone, or Add photos in the toolbar to fill several zones at once. On a computer you can also drag photos from a folder straight onto the page.
3. Drag inside a photo to re-centre it. Pinch, scroll-wheel, or the Zoom slider to crop.
4. Click a text zone and write as much or as little as you want.
5. Save as… once to choose a project file; after that Ctrl/⌘+S writes straight back to it, and it saves itself every minute.

Your project file is the real copy. The browser keeps a spare of your work and your template library, and clearing your browsing data erases those — but it can never touch a file you have saved. Templates also ride along inside project files, and Back up templates writes them to a file of their own.

Runs entirely on this computer. No accounts, no uploads, no internet — your photos and writing never leave this machine.
On a phone or tablet: ☰ opens the pages, and selecting a zone brings up Edit this zone at the bottom right for all its settings.
