Scrapbook Press

Welcome to Scrapbook Press, a free tool for arranging photos and text for easy exports into booklet publications.

I’ve designed the website to work by saving projects as a folder on your local desktop — a json file with your layout and writing, plus a copy of each photo — that is pulled into the editor and saved automatically every minute. Use “Save project as…” and select an empty folder as the reference point for the save. After, you can press “Save project” to manually save or press ctrl+s. Then, on your next startup a pop-up will ask if you want to continue your last project or begin a new one.

This avoids any sign in to an external database, so nothing outside your local machine holds your photobook.

The program comes with about a dozen templates of pages and you can start and save your own for future use.

Please feel free to contact me for any feedback.
